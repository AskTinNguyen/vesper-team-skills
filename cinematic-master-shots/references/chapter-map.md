# Master Shots Chapter Map (Kenworthy)

Source: Christopher Kenworthy, *Master Shots* Volumes 1-3. Chapter titles below are taken from each book's contents. Volume 1's contents is a dense two-column page in the source; the setup-level titles listed are the readable chapter-family names, so use the book's own contents pages for the exact setup numbering.

## Volume 1 - High-impact moves by scene family

1. Fight Scenes
2. Chase Scenes
3. Entrances & Exits
4. Suspense, Searching & Creeping
5. Dramatic Shift
6. Revelations & Discoveries
7. Shock Horror
8. Directing Attention
9. Car Scenes
10. Dialogue Scenes
11. Arguments & Conflict
12. Love & Sex Scenes

Fight setups include Long Lens Stunt, Speed Punch, Matching Motion, Knock Down, Cutting for Impact, Down on the Floor, Off-Screen Violence, and The Moment of Defeat. Chase setups include Travel with Subject, Long Lens Pan, Passing Through Tight Spaces, Through Open Spaces, Surprises Along the Way, The Unseen Attacker, The Closing Attacker, Unfair Speed Gain, Almost There, and Footwork.

## Volume 2 - Dialogue scenes

1. Conflict
2. Increasing Tension (Circling Dialogue)
3. Power Struggles
4. Group Conversation
5. Revealing Plot
6. Walking and Talking
7. Intense Emotions (Argument in Motion)
8. Intimacy
9. Long Distance
10. Creative Staging

Volume 2 is devoted to shooting great dialogue: block first, avoid the default static angle and reverse-angle, and let the camera intensify plot and emotion rather than just record a conversation.

## Volume 3 - The Director's Vision

1. Advanced Lensing
2. Essential Motion (Blended Moves, Reveal Moves, Short Moves)
3. Depth Staging (Character Moves)
4. Expert Framing
5. Symbolic Staging
6. Production Design and Camera
7. Dynamic Action (Action Shift)
8. Shooting Performance
9. Camera Height
10. Complex Camera Moves
11. The Advanced Director (Scene Staging)

Volume 3 emphasizes matching the move to the lens and space, staging depth and character, and knowing when a disciplined simpler setup outperforms an elaborate one.
