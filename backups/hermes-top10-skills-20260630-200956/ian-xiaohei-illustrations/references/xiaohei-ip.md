# Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei is the recurring visual IP of Ian-style Chinese in-article illustrations.

Xiaohei should appear in every default image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration. Xiaohei is an absurd worker seriously participating in a system operation.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White circular dot eyes.
- Thin legs, sometimes thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline with hand-drawn feeling.
- Blank, dazed, calm, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a quiet system operator.
- Dry humor, not cute performance.
- A little clumsy, but not stupid.
- Feels truly responsible for a job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common responsibilities

Make Xiaohei perform the core action:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling lines to gather information sources.
- Getting stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operating a “judgment” lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting open a “material fish”.
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Pulling a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign while looking at pitfalls.
- Reaching out from a hole but failing to catch content.
- Carrying bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, recording.

## Bans

- Do not make Xiaohei overly cute.
- Do not make Xiaohei a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothes, meme expressions, or shiny eyes.
- Do not place Xiaohei in a corner just watching.
- Do not let Xiaohei steal the whole structural meaning.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, too round, or too polished.

## Judgment standard

If the image's core metaphor still works perfectly after removing Xiaohei, Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the action subject.
