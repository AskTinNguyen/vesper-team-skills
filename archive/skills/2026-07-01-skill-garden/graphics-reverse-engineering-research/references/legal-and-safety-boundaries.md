# Legal and Safety Boundaries

This skill supports legitimate graphics and reverse-engineering research. It must not be used to facilitate illegal access, DRM bypass, piracy, cheating, credential theft, or redistribution of proprietary assets.

## Allowed / safer uses

- Analyzing the user's own code, shaders, assets, captures, or binaries.
- Studying open-source projects, public documentation, permissively licensed datasets, or toy examples.
- Debugging rendering artifacts in a project the user is authorized to inspect.
- Interoperability, preservation, accessibility, security review, or documentation work within legal limits.
- High-level educational explanation of techniques such as POM, tessellation, compute shaders, screen-space displacement, atomics, culling, or WebGPU prototypes.

## Disallowed or high-risk requests

Refuse or redirect requests that ask for:

- Circumventing DRM, anti-cheat, access controls, encryption, or licensing checks.
- Creating cheats, bots, wallhacks, aim assists, online exploit tooling, or ban-evasion workflows.
- Extracting, cloning, or redistributing proprietary assets without permission.
- Obtaining secrets, credentials, private keys, account tokens, or non-public service data.
- Bypassing payment, subscriptions, region locks, or entitlement checks.
- Evading detection by game/platform security systems.

## If authorization is unclear

Ask for the minimal clarification required, or proceed only with:

- public docs and open-source examples,
- toy data generated for the explanation,
- user-provided excerpts that do not reveal secrets,
- methodology-level planning without operational bypass steps.

## Safe wording pattern

Use:

> I can help analyze authorized shaders/assets and build a research workflow or toy reproduction. I can't help bypass DRM/anti-cheat or extract proprietary assets without permission.

## Reporting hygiene

Final reports should separate:

- What was observed from public/user-authorized evidence.
- What was inferred.
- What remains uncertain.
- Which outputs are safe to share.
- Which files/assets should not be redistributed.
