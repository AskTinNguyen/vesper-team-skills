# The Hero's Journey (Campbell)

Source: Joseph Campbell, *The Hero With a Thousand Faces*. Use as a diagnostic map for transformation-driven heroes, not as a sequence-by-sequence template.

## Part I: The Adventure of the Hero

### Departure

1. The Call to Adventure: the hero's normal world is disturbed by a summons.
2. Refusal of the Call: the hero resists or makes excuses.
3. Supernatural Aid: a mentor/guide/talisman prepares the hero for the threshold.
4. The Crossing of the First Threshold: the hero leaves the known world and commits to the adventure.
5. The Belly of the Whale: the hero is swallowed / passes a point of no return and begins transformation.

### Initiation

1. The Road of Trials: a series of tests, allies, and enemies that harden the hero.
2. The Meeting with the Goddess: a peak of realized potential or union with the source.
3. Woman as the Temptress: the hero faces temptation that could sidetrack the quest.
4. Atonement with the Father: the hero confronts and reconciles with the authority that stands between them and the boon; the key "killing the father" moment.
5. Apotheosis: the hero reaches a state of expanded understanding; is more than mortal self.
6. The Ultimate Boon: the hero obtains the thing they crossed the threshold to win.

### Return

1. Refusal of the Return: the hero does not want to bring the boon back to the ordinary world.
2. The Magic Flight: the hero flees or is pursued with the boon.
3. Rescue from Without: an ally or power helps the hero get home.
4. The Crossing of the Return Threshold: the hero re-enters the ordinary world and translates the boon into it.
5. Master of the Two Worlds: the hero moves freely between adventure and ordinary life.
6. Freedom to Live: the hero achieves a state of inner freedom and is not afraid of life or death.

## Part II: The Cosmogonic Cycle (optional depth)

- Emanations: creation from the void into the manifold.
- The Virgin Birth: miraculous or symbolic origin of the hero.
- Transformations of the Hero: the archetypal shapes the hero takes (warrior, lover, emperor/tyrant, redeemer, saint).
- Dissolutions: the end of worlds and the death-and-rebirth of forms.

## Practical Mapping to a Screenplay

1. Map Departure onto Act 1: call, refusal, aid, first threshold, point of no return.
2. Map Initiation onto Act 2's escalating complications through to the boon.
3. Map Return onto Act 3: reluctance, flight/stakes, crossing back, applying the lesson, integration.
4. Verify the hero is genuinely changed and the ordinary world is transformed by the return; a heroic quest that ends in the hero simply going back unchanged is unresolved.
