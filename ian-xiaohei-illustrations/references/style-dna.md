# Style DNA

## One sentence

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, airy, restrained, weird, product-sketch-like, handwritten Chinese feeling, structurally clear but not instructional.

It should feel like an AI/product/design/developer-tools person casually drew an explanatory sketch on a white sheet of paper.

## Must-have rules

- 16:9 horizontal in-article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector-clean, not thick outlined.
- Lots of empty space: subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas; at least 35% blank space, ideally one quiet open area.
- Sparse handwritten Chinese annotations: at most 5-8 notes, each ideally 2-8 Chinese characters.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Let the structure emerge naturally. Do not write the structure type on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line art, character, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional points, critical reminders, outcomes.
- Orange: main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, A-to-B movement.
- Blue: supporting notes, mental state, system state, second-layer explanations, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Use color with restraint. Less is better than more.

## Absolute bans

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute cartoon poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI look.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Do not explain every node.
- Do not write type titles like “Workflow”, “System Architecture”, “Common Pitfalls”, or “Roadmap” in the top-left.

## Aesthetic direction

The image should be weird, creative, interesting, clean, airy, and imaginative.

It should not be cute, childish, overly complex, or rigid.
