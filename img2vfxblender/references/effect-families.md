# Effect-family decisions

Choose behavior before texture or palette. These are candidate Blender implementations, not claims about the source game. Preserve source-specific features in the contract.

| Study IDs | Family and essential read | Candidate build and temporal checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SP-01 | Barrier: coherent wall and continuous ground seam | Segmented plane/curved surface with noise-masked emission/opacity; coarse falling strips plus sparse grains. Rise once, seal once, quiet hold, controlled release. Do not let surface noise create false holes. |
| SP-02 | Portal: readable rim/opening, inward readiness, outward accepted-use beat | Curve/annular mesh, tapered spiral strands and sparse grains. Distinguish loop from traversal pulse; test oblique opening and cancellation. Refraction remains optional. |
| MV-01 | Quick departure: compact gather and old-position residue | A small contracting ring/glint and coarse falling grains. Freeze the departure transform before the actor moves. |
| MV-02 / MV-03 | Sand departure and arrival: directional body transition | Inward collapse then coarse/fine falling residue for departure; destination pre-roll then reveal/outward pulse for arrival. Gravity-driven particles are not blindly reversed. |
| MV-04 | Rift departure: vertical slit closes; bright grains fade before darker fall | Narrowing ribbon/mesh slit, lateral grains and staged material fades. Fix popping at opacity/lifetime rather than covering it with a second full-screen flash. |
| MV-05 | Temporary stairs: stable tread edges and controlled dissolve | Editable step mesh, subdued surface flow, rim accents and rising dissolve dust. Traversability is a separate gameplay contract. |
| MV-06 | Body charge: convergence and one clear handoff | Contracting ring plus curved strands, sparse points and one glint. Variable-duration charge needs hold/cancel; a one-shot should not imply an indefinite loop. |
| MV-07 | Dash body: moving silhouette, restrained echo | Body proxy plus short sampled transforms/material envelope. Do not copy red = armor into NineTails. Test attachment, end/cancel and old-position echoes. |
| MV-08 | Trail or launch burst: classification unresolved | Inspect footage before committing to a sustained ribbon. With stills, deliver a labeled launch-cue study or two clearly proposed alternatives; never claim the source trail duration is known. |
| CB-01 | Radial release: boundary and center without obscuring next action | Expanding ring with low dust and sparse granular edge. Radius comes from the intended mechanic; victim impact is separate. |
| CB-02 | Strong signal: warning versus impact unresolved | Compact glint, converging arcs and broken ring as a visual study. Defer event binding until evidence or design resolves the role. |
| CB-03 | Response signal: distinct bilateral shape and center beat | Paired curved strands plus focal signal. Proposed palette must follow the project's combat vocabulary. Never infer a parry window from flare lifetime. |
| CB-04 | Stagger: brief break exposing the vulnerable pose | Compact outward burst and a few shards; test repeated hits and quick clear. Distinguish from shield break. |
| CB-05 | Shield: persistent protection silhouette, one decisive break | Rim/shell plus quiet flow; breakup fragments detach from sampled surface positions. Expiry, dispel and actual break need distinct owner events. |
| CB-06 | Death: modest collapse and body cleanup | Proxy death/removal, low inward wisps and small accent. Test simultaneous deaths, early corpse removal and pooled material reset at engine handoff. |

The low-detail fixtures exercise SP-02, MV-06 and CB-05 because they cover a space loop, a burst and a loop-to-break transition. They are deliberately interpretable; the other thirteen studies are contract/routing examples until their own scenes are authored and reviewed.
